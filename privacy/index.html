Privacy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

We collect nothing. DOGS ID asks one question — are you old enough — and remembers your answer on your own device. That is the entire data model.

  • No birthdays. We never ask for or store your date of birth.
  • No accounts. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to log into.
  • No tracking. No analytics, no third-party scripts, no fingerprinting.
  • No cookies. This page sets zero cookies.
  • Device-only memory. Your yes/no answer is saved in your browser’s localStorage, one key per age threshold. It never leaves your device and no DOGS server ever sees it. Clear your site data and the gate simply asks again.

Embedded gates on DOGS sites follow the same rules: a yes/no flag on the visitor’s device, nothing sent anywhere.

Questions about this policy: reach us through wearedogs.net.
